In this post, we analyze the performance of **Forward+ and various Cluster Culling algorithms** in detail, across different grid resolutions **from 8x8 to 128x128**.
The benchmarks were conducted on a scene with only opaque objects, illuminated by **1024 point lights and 1024 spot lights**!
It is important to note that these **lights were randomly distributed around the Sponza scene**, meaning not all of them were visible on-screen at the same time!!!
A **fixed pseudo-random seed** was used to ensure that every technique was benchmarked against the exact same light configuration.
All benchmarks were executed on an **Intel Core i5-13600K CPU** paired with an **NVIDIA RTX 4060 GPU**.
These measurements were crucial during the optimization of the Synapse Engine's architecture.
